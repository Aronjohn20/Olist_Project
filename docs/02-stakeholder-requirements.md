# Stakeholder Requirements

> Status: Draft — Requirements Gathering in Progress

## Stakeholder

Primary stakeholders:

- Marketplace Managers
- Seller Operations Team

## Initial Requirements

The system should help stakeholders:

- Identify high-performing and underperforming sellers.
- Compare sellers based on relevant performance measures.
- Understand why a seller may be underperforming.
- Analyze seller performance over time.
- Investigate seller performance at different levels of detail.

## Initial Performance Dimensions

Potential dimensions include:

- Sales performance
- Customer experience
- Delivery performance
- Product/category performance

## Initial KPIs

Potential KPIs include:

- Revenue
- Order count
- Items sold
- Average Order Value
- Average review score
- Poor-review percentage
- Average delivery time
- Late delivery rate

## Initial Analysis Levels

The system may support analysis at:

- Marketplace level
- Category level
- Seller level

## Initial Dashboard Requirements

The final analytical interface may include:

- Overall marketplace performance
- Seller comparison
- Top and bottom performing sellers
- Seller drill-down
- Category analysis
- Time-based trends
- Filters for relevant business dimensions

## Important

These requirements are not yet considered final.

Requirements will be refined through stakeholder interviews and technical analysis during the project.
