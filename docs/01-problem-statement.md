# Problem Statement

## Business Problem

The marketplace has a large number of sellers, but does not have a centralized analytical system to identify high-performing and underperforming sellers or understand the factors affecting seller performance.

Seller information is distributed across different datasets, making it difficult for marketplace management to get a complete view of seller performance.

## Business Need

Marketplace managers need a reliable analytical system that brings together seller, order, product, payment, review, and delivery-related information.

The system should help management:

- Identify sellers that require attention.
- Compare seller performance.
- Understand the factors affecting seller performance.
- Track seller performance over time.
- Support data-driven operational decisions.

## Project Objective

Build a centralized Seller Performance & Marketplace Operations Analytics Platform that transforms raw marketplace data into reliable, business-ready analytical data and presents the results through an interactive dashboard.

## Primary Stakeholders

- Marketplace Managers
- Seller Operations Team

## Secondary Users

- Business Analysts
- Data Analysts
